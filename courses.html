Our Courses

Five exam programmes, taught by Mahidol Math graduates, for students of every level.

Primary & Secondary

UK Math

Covers Key Stage 2 through Key Stage 4 mathematics following the UK National Curriculum. Builds strong arithmetic, algebra and geometry foundations, with regular topic tests modelled on UK school assessments.

Primary – High School

US Math

Grade-level mathematics aligned to US Common Core standards, from elementary arithmetic through Algebra I & II, Geometry and Pre-Calculus, with homework support and quiz preparation.

Ages 14–16

IGCSE Mathematics

Cambridge and Edexcel IGCSE Math, Core and Extended tiers. Focuses on past paper technique, common mark-scheme pitfalls, and building speed and accuracy under exam conditions.

Ages 16–18

A-Level Mathematics

Pure Mathematics, Statistics and Mechanics for AS and A2 Level. Designed for students aiming for top grades needed for competitive university admissions.

College Prep

SAT Math

Full SAT Math preparation covering algebra, problem solving, data analysis and advanced math sections, with full-length timed practice tests and score tracking.

Class Formats

Every course above is available in the following formats.

1-on-1 Private

Fully personalised pace and topic focus, ideal for catching up or aiming for top grades.

Small Group (2–4)

Peer discussion and collaborative problem-solving, kept small for individual attention.

Exam Bootcamp

Intensive short courses before major exams, focused entirely on past papers and technique.
